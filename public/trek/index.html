day 67 · sofia · 28 november 2019

1,982 km, walked.

Fifty-two days on foot, and one whole autumn.

after sixty-seven days

He got the coach from Sofia to Istanbul.

He was done.

Done with walking — not with moving. Istanbul: two weeks and 157 km of running with local crews on both sides of the Bosphorus. Rishikesh by late December, a first run in India along the Ganges. Goa in the new year, barefoot sprints on Calangute beach. Home in Otley by 16 January — a “Welcome Home Run”, and a 10k with Otley AC the same day.

It had started nine months before Paris, in Greenwich Park, with an experiment in sitting somewhere with nothing — a choir rehearsing in the Old Naval College chapel, “a sort of future-formed nostalgia.” Nine months later he walked out of the airport instead of flying home.
