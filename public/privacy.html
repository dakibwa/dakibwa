Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-16

This privacy policy explains how Akibwa handles data when used for personal developer integrations, including health and wellness integrations such as WHOOP.

What data may be accessed

If you authorize an integration, Akibwa may access account data that you explicitly grant via OAuth scopes. For WHOOP, this can include profile information, sleep data, recovery data, cycle data, workout data, and body measurements.

How the data is used

Authorized data is used only to provide personal analytics, summaries, automations, and assistant features requested by the account owner.

Data sharing

Data accessed through these integrations is not sold. It is not shared with third parties except as needed to operate the authorized integration or where required by law.

Data storage

Data may be processed and stored in private infrastructure controlled by the operator of Akibwa for personal use, including local tools, scripts, and secure service providers used to run the assistant.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time through the connected provider, such as your WHOOP account or developer authorization settings.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact dakibwa@gmail.com.
