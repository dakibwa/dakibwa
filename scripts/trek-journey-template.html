About this journey

Paris → Sofia. 24 September to 28 November 2019.

Recorded route, gently rounded for the view.

Lighter dashed lines join gaps for a continuous journey. They are visual connections, not recorded walking.

The camera follows the direction of travel above the landscape. Photographs belong to a day; their exact position is not known. Terrain is shown at its real height.

The landscape is drawn as paper. Trees illustrate mapped woodland; buildings follow mapped footprints with simplified heights and roofs. The map shows today’s geographic context, alongside the 2019 route and photographs. Named landmarks are little architectural interpretations at mapped locations; their forms and heights are simplified. Their appearance along the route does not imply a visit inside.

Distance and ascent come from the original day totals. Within-day counters are estimates; visual connections add neither distance nor ascent.

The elevation profile follows the whole journey. Heights are approximate mapped ground elevation from Mapzen, sampled about every 200 metres, rather than recorded GPS altitude. Gaps stay unmeasured. The current height is separate from accumulated metres climbed.
